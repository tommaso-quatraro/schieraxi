What it does

It gets you to the deadline knowing who to field.

  1. One number per player

    The expected rating: what he can score in that match, if he plays.

  2. The best lineup

    Your eleven, formation and bench order, with your league's rules.

  3. The reason for every choice

    See the reasons and how sure the advice is. Then you decide.

The expected rating

How many points you can expect from a player in that match.

Not the season average: the opponent, role, form and who takes penalties all count.

Then we look at how likely he is to play. Together they give the expected points.

Get started

  1. Open the web app

    Create an account. It works in the browser, on phone and computer.

  2. Bring your league

    Enter rules and squads. Squads can be imported from a file or a text.

  3. You line them up

    Before each matchday read the advice and send the lineup where you play.

Open the web app

On iPhone you can add it to the Home Screen, or install the app with SideStore. App Store and Google Play: soon.

Install the iPhone app with SideStore

You need an iPhone with iOS 18 or newer, an Apple ID and Wi-Fi. The official SideStore guide (sidestore.io) is always the most up to date.

  1. Install SideStore by following its guide (Nightly version).
  2. In SideStore open Sources, tap + and paste:
    https://github.com/tommaso-quatraro/schieraxi/releases/latest/download/sorgente.json
  3. Choose SchieraXI and tap Install. If asked, pick “Register App ID for Each Extension”: the widget needs it.
  4. Open the app, type the address https://app.schieraxi.it and sign in.

With a free Apple ID the app must be renewed every 7 days: SideStore can do it by itself at night. Alerts come from the web app.

Frequently asked questions

Does SchieraXI submit my lineup for me?

No. It recommends the eleven, formation and bench. You send the lineup yourself, where you play.

Which leagues does it work with?

“Classic” fantacalcio leagues on Serie A, with the rules you set: bonuses, maluses, formations. You enter squads and rules yourself.

How reliable is the advice?

It is an estimate, and it says so: next to the expected points you see how uncertain the advice is. The model is tested on past matchdays, but football stays unpredictable.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, the web app works in the browser on both. For iPhone there is also an app, installed with SideStore. Store apps will come later.

What about my data?

No ads, no tracking, no odds or betting. This site uses no cookies: it only remembers the theme you pick. Squads and choices stay in your account, which you can delete whenever you want from the app. Details in the privacy policy.
