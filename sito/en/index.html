The recommended lineup

For classic Serie A fantasy football (fantacalcio): it estimates each player's expected fantasy score and suggests the eleven, formation and bench under your league's rules.

Web app by invitation. iPhone app coming, Android planned.

How it works

Before each matchday the app works out three things. You then enter the lineup yourself, on the site where your league plays.

Expected fantasy score
The rating with bonuses and penalties a player can get in that match, if he plays. It depends on the opponent, role, form and who takes the penalties.
Chance of playing
How likely he is to be on the pitch, from the predicted lineups and recent appearances.
Recommended lineup
The eleven, formation and bench order with the most expected points, within your league's rules.

Questions

Does it enter the lineup for me?

No. It suggests the eleven, formation and bench; you enter the lineup yourself, on the site where your league plays.

Which leagues does it work with?

Classic leagues on Serie A. Bonuses, penalties, formations and bench are set in the league rules.

How reliable is the advice?

It is an estimate. Next to the expected points the app shows how uncertain the advice is. The model is checked against past matchdays.

How do I get in?

For now with an invitation code, which expires after 7 days. The person who invites you gives you the code.

What data does it collect?

Only what the account needs: login details, squads, rules and choices. No ads and no tracking; this site uses no cookies. You can delete the account from the app. Details in the privacy notice.
